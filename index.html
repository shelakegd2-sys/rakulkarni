About

Dr. Ravindra Ashok Kulkarni is an Assistant Professor in the Department of English at Willingdon College, Sangli. He has more than twenty-two years of teaching experience and is recognized as a Postgraduate Lecturer and Ph.D. Research Guide in English Language and Literature.

His academic interests include English Language and Literature, American Literature, British Literature, Cultural Studies, Translation Studies, Linguistics, Marathi Literature, Indian Knowledge Systems, Philosophy and Humanities.

He has actively contributed to higher education through research, teacher training, academic administration, curriculum development, resource lectures and student mentoring.
